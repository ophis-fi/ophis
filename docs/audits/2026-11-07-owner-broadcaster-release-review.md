## Residual risks

Baal administrators or a compromised host can access the unlocked software gas key; the owner explicitly accepted this custody model. Private builders and trusted simulation endpoints remain trust assumptions. Included outer reverts/cancellations can consume operator gas without fee reimbursement. Collected private fees require owner-controlled withdrawal/conversion/refill and are not automatically spendable native gas. No new paid service, new key, additional reserve allocation beyond the owner's 0.002 BNB approval, or other-chain public activation is part of this release.
