Swap on Robinhood Chain

Ophis is an intent-based DEX aggregator with a trading interface for Robinhood Chain, network ID 4663. Choose a supported token pair, review the available quote and sign an order with your own wallet. Solvers compete to settle the order through batch auctions.

Open Robinhood Chain swap

Ophis is independent of Robinhood Markets. This is an on-chain swap interface, not a Robinhood brokerage account or a stock redemption service.

Before you sign

  1. Choose the network and tokens. The button above selects Robinhood Chain. Confirm the network shown in the app and wallet, then select the tokens and amount. Verify unfamiliar token contracts against their issuer's official information.
  2. Wait for an executable quote. A listed token does not guarantee liquidity for your amount. If no route is available, do not infer a price or fill from the token list.
  3. Check the minimum received. Compare the quoted output with the minimum in the order you sign. MEV protection mitigates common front-running and sandwich attacks; it does not eliminate price, token or contract risk.
  4. Approve only what is needed, then sign. A token allowance transaction may be required before the swap order. Review the spender and allowance in your wallet. Follow the order status until settlement or expiry; signing is not confirmation of a fill.

What fees apply?

Ophis charges a 0.01% base fee plus capped reference-quote-improvement capture. Approval or other on-chain transactions can require network gas. Review the current fee policy and the actual quote before signing; a gasless order signature does not mean every step is free.

Can I swap tokenized stocks?

Only where the app supports the token and can return an executable quote. Tokenized-stock access, transfer restrictions and redemption rights depend on the issuer and your eligibility. A ticker is not proof that a token is the asset you intend to trade.

Is this a bridge from another chain?

This entry point opens a swap on Robinhood Chain. It does not move your balance from Ethereum, Base or another network. Check the selected source and destination before using any separate cross-chain route.
