Ophis retired Unichain (chain ID 130) on 19 November 2026. New swaps, bridge routes and agent orders on this network are no longer supported. This page replaces the former trading guide so old links do not lead users to an unavailable market.

Historical trades, fee accounting and deployment records are retained. Recovery metadata for Circle transfers sent before retirement remains available. Do not send new funds to a retired Ophis route.

See the [current supported networks](https://docs.ophis.fi/networks-assets) and [service status](https://docs.ophis.fi/status). A replacement chain will be considered after the Ophis security, code and development cleanup.
