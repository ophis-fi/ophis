Recovery and pending transactions
        

          Recovery requires native currency for gas. Funding a temporary account from your public wallet creates a public link.
        

        

        
        

        
        
        
        
        
        

          Canceling invalidates the old swap authorization; it does not move or recover tokens. The temporary account
          needs native currency for gas. Funding it from your public wallet creates a public link.
        

        
        
        
        

          Resuming reuses the saved withdrawal proof so it cannot withdraw twice. Your public wallet pays gas, the
          committed broadcaster fee still applies, and the transaction becomes linked to that wallet.
