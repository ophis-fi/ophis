# FAQ

Find the wallet, network and order requirements for your Ophis swap.

## Trading questions

<details id="how-is-ophis-different-from-1inch-or-matcha">
<summary>How is Ophis different from 1inch or Matcha?</summary>

Ophis public batch-auction orders use solver competition and signed price limits. Direct swaps, private swaps and bridges have separate execution flows. You authorize the trade in your wallet, and solvers find a route within those limits. Compare the final amount received for the same trade.

</details>

<details id="do-i-need-to-connect-a-wallet">
<summary>Do I need to connect a wallet?</summary>

Connect an EVM wallet for batch-auction orders. For an external NEAR source, use your source wallet to fund the quoted deposit and provide a source-chain refund address. Standard ERC-20 orders keep funds in your wallet until settlement.

</details>

<details id="which-networks-are-supported">
<summary>Which networks are supported?</summary>

The swap app and CLI support 14 EVM chains on mainnet, plus the Sepolia testnet. The standalone app also accepts NEAR Intents sources and destinations.

| Interface | Networks |
| --- | --- |
| App and CLI | 15 EVM networks, including Sepolia |
| EVM mainnets | Ethereum, Arbitrum One, Avalanche, Base, BNB Smart Chain, Gnosis Chain, Ink, Linea, Optimism, Plasma, Polygon, Robinhood Chain, Unichain, Arc |
| NEAR Intents | Bitcoin, Solana, Monad, X Layer, Sui, Tron, Hyperliquid (Hypercore), Starknet, Zcash |
| SDK v0.4.5 and hosted MCP | 14 EVM mainnets, including Arc |

</details>

<details id="is-my-trade-protected-from-mev">
<summary>Is my trade protected from MEV?</summary>

Batch auctions use offchain order submission and uniform clearing prices to reduce front-running and sandwich opportunities. This describes the batch-auction path; direct swaps, private swaps and bridges have different protections and risks. The settlement contract enforces your signed limits.

</details>

<details id="what-happens-if-no-solver-matches-my-intent">
<summary>What happens if no solver matches my intent?</summary>

An unfilled standard ERC-20 order expires, leaving unsold funds in your wallet. Native-token deposits require an onchain refund, and bridge routes use provider recovery. Soft cancellation can race an in-flight settlement.

| Order setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Default validity window | 30 minutes |

</details>

<details id="who-runs-the-solvers">
<summary>Who runs the solvers?</summary>

The solver operator depends on the network. Ophis runs the solver on Optimism, Unichain, Robinhood Chain and Arc; partner-operated solver networks handle the other public batch-auction chains. The onchain allowlist controls which solvers can settle orders, and settlement enforces your signed limit price.

| Ophis-operated chains |
| --- |
| Optimism, Unichain, Robinhood Chain, Arc |

</details>

<details id="how-are-partner-fees-collected">
<summary>How are partner fees collected?</summary>

Batch-auction orders carry Ophis's **1 bp base**. The app and SDK put the base fee in appData. The Ophis backend applies improvement capture on operated chains; appData carries both components on partner-hosted chains.

| Ophis fee component | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Base | 1 bp |
| Volatile-pair improvement capture | 80%, capped at 99 bps |
| Stable-pair improvement capture | 50%, capped at 20 bps |

</details>

<details id="is-ophis-open-source">
<summary>Is Ophis open source?</summary>

Yes. The frontend, intent-parser proxy and infrastructure runbooks are public at github.com/ophis-fi/ophis.

</details>

<details id="can-i-use-ophiss-intent-parser-in-my-own-app">
<summary>Can I use Ophis's intent parser in my own app?</summary>

Yes. Use the public API to turn natural-language requests into structured trade fields for your app or agent.

| API setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Endpoint | POST /api/intent |
| Rate limit | 30 req/min/IP |
| Authentication | No auth or key required |

</details>

## Private swaps

**Private** mode uses a RAILGUN shielded wallet on Ethereum, Arbitrum, Polygon and BNB Chain. Switching modes does not shield your public balance. Deposits must synchronize and become **Spendable** before use. The Ophis private-swap fee is 0.1% of output, with pool, broadcaster and applicable deposit or withdrawal costs in addition. See [Networks & assets](/networks-assets#private-swaps) for funding and recovery.
